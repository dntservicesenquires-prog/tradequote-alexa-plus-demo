# Demo video script (about 90 seconds)

**0:00–0:10 — Introduce the problem**  
“Tradespeople often work out prices across several jobs and trades. TradeQuote turns a plain-language job description into a draft they can review before sending.”

**0:10–0:35 — Single-trade quote**  
Tap **Try a sample bathroom quote**. Show Jane Smith’s job, the extracted hours and rate, materials, markup, no VAT, deposit, and calculated total. Change one value to show the total update. Say that the figures stay editable and are examples.

**0:35–1:05 — Multi-trade project**  
Tap **Start a multi-trade project**. Show the plumbing, tiling, and decorating stages. Change one stage amount and show the combined quote and deposit update.

**1:05–1:20 — Save and output**  
Save the draft, show it in the saved list, and briefly show the print-to-PDF action.

**1:20–1:30 — Explain the build**  
“This is a browser-based Alexa+ style simulation, not an Amazon integration. It runs locally, needs no API key, and keeps drafts in this browser. The new work is the conversational quote flow and the editable multi-trade project estimate.”

Keep the final recording under three minutes, use English narration or captions, and show the project itself rather than third-party footage or music.
